# Roblox Development & Game Design: top 20 videos

How these were picked: yt-dlp search across 6 queries for this topic, about 25 results each, Shorts removed. Videos were ranked by reach (log of views) and relevance (how many queries returned them, and how high), then hand-checked for topic fit. View counts are from Nov 2026.

How much to trust each takeaway: **0/20** are well-documented talks or essays, summarised from what they're known to cover. The other 20 are **from title + description**: YouTube blocked transcript downloads from the cloud session, so read those as hypotheses until you've watched the video or run `research/tools/fetch_transcripts.py` on your own computer.

## Recurring patterns across the 20

- **Scope small, ship fast, iterate on data.** Nearly every success story is a tightly-scoped game launched in days/weeks, then improved weekly using stats.
- **80% proven / 20% novel.** Reuse genre conventions players already understand (loops, UI layout, progression); spend novelty on theme and one twist.
- **Stats drive the algorithm.** Session length, D1/D7 retention, play-through rate and monetization are what Home recommendations reward; effort or graphics alone don't.
- **Packaging is half the game.** Thumbnails, icon and title determine whether anyone tries the game; test many variants.
- **First games fail; that's the curriculum.** Creators frame flops as practice and recommend many small releases over one giant project.
- **Instrument from day one.** Funnels on onboarding and the core loop are the fastest way to find what's broken.
