# Player Retention: top 20 videos

How these were picked: yt-dlp search across 6 queries for this topic, about 25 results each, Shorts removed. Videos were ranked by reach (log of views) and relevance (how many queries returned them, and how high), then hand-checked for topic fit. View counts are from Nov 2026.

How much to trust each takeaway: **6/20** are well-documented talks or essays, summarised from what they're known to cover. The other 14 are **from title + description**: YouTube blocked transcript downloads from the cloud session, so read those as hypotheses until you've watched the video or run `research/tools/fetch_transcripts.py` on your own computer.

## Recurring patterns across the 20

- **Fun in the first minute, payoff within five.** Every source stresses an immediate first action, an early win, and a visible next goal; Roblox's own guidance targets reaching the fun within ~5 minutes.
- **Teach by doing.** Short contextual prompts (arrows, beams, highlights) beat text tutorials; funnel every tutorial step and fix the worst one.
- **Give a concrete reason to return tomorrow.** Something growing/finishing while away, an escalating daily reward, a friend's progress - tied to the real goal, not just free currency.
- **D1 = onboarding, D7 = progression, D30 = content and social.** Diagnose retention by phase instead of adding daily rewards everywhere.
- **Emotional > rational hooks.** Attachment (pets, bases, identity), mastery and social belonging hold players longer than reward schedules alone.
- **Avoid compulsion traps.** Loss aversion, sunk-cost pressure and punishing absence raise short-term numbers but damage trust; respect stopping points.
