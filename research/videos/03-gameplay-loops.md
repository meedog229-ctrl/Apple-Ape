# Gameplay Loops: top 20 videos

How these were picked: yt-dlp search across 6 queries for this topic, about 25 results each, Shorts removed. Videos were ranked by reach (log of views) and relevance (how many queries returned them, and how high), then hand-checked for topic fit. View counts are from Nov 2026.

How much to trust each takeaway: **9/20** are well-documented talks or essays, summarised from what they're known to cover. The other 11 are **from title + description**: YouTube blocked transcript downloads from the cloud session, so read those as hypotheses until you've watched the video or run `research/tools/fetch_transcripts.py` on your own computer.

## Recurring patterns across the 20

- **Three nested loops.** Moment-to-moment action (seconds) → session goals (minutes) → long-term progression (days); each layer needs its own payoff.
- **Write the loop as a sentence:** action → feedback/reward → progression choice → more expressive or harder action. If you can't, the design isn't clear yet.
- **Progression must change play, not just numbers.** New zones, tools, mechanics and abilities beat +X% stat treadmills.
- **Economies need sinks.** Every currency source needs a matching drain (upgrades, rebirths, cosmetics) or inflation kills reward value.
- **Prestige/rebirth loops** let simulator/incremental games extend content cheaply: reset progress for a permanent multiplier and a new layer.
- **Prove the core action is fun first.** Prototype the repeated action in isolation before building content or meta systems.
